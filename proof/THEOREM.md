\[
Z(P(n,3))=8.
\]

Here `P(n,3)` is the generalized Petersen graph with vertices `u_i,v_i` indexed modulo `n` and edges

\[
u_i u_{i+1},\qquad u_i v_i,\qquad v_i v_{i+3}.
\]
